# Glossary

**Keep or Delete**: an iPhone app to clean the photo gallery by swiping through photos one by one.

- **Swipe**: a finger gesture on a photo card. Right means keep, left means delete.
- **Keep**: the photo stays in the gallery.
- **Delete**: the photo is marked to be removed from the gallery.
- **Decisions**: every keep or delete choice so far, saved on the phone so it survives a restart.
- **Spared**: a photo marked for delete but unchecked on the review screen; it counts as kept.
- **Group**: a set of photos to go through on its own, picked on the start screen (screenshots, videos,
  a month, ...). A photo can be in several groups; decided in one, it does not show in another.
- **To go**: photos in a group with no decision yet.
- **Photo key**: creation time in whole milliseconds plus width and height, for example
  `1700000000123:4032x3024`. Phone and Mac ids differ, so this is how a photo is found on both sides.
- **Mac match**: a phone photo whose photo key exactly one Mac photo has.
